# The System — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The System is a workout log that runs on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. Optional purchases (such as the Monarch Pass) are cosmetic and go through Google Play.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your device, in the app's private storage:

- workouts, sets, routines, programs and schedule
- optional measurements (such as bodyweight), readiness, sleep, water and food entries
- optional progress photos, saved in the app's private folder after location and camera details are removed
- game progress (XP, rank, quests, items) and your settings

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to Google Drive.

## What leaves your device, and only when you choose

- **Backups and exports.** You can create an encrypted backup or export JSON/CSV files. They go only where you send them with Android's share sheet. Photos are included in a backup only if you turn that on.
- **Share cards.** An image is made only when you tap Share. You choose every field on the card first. Bodyweight, measurements, notes, photos and location are never on a card.
- **AI coach (optional).** If you add your own Anthropic API key, the question you type and the training summary you approve are sent directly from your phone to Anthropic when you tap Ask. You can read the exact text first. Anthropic's own privacy policy covers that request. Your key is kept in Android's secure storage.
- **JARVIS link (optional).** If you pair the app with your own JARVIS computer, it sends summaries of finished workouts (title, date, sets, reps, minutes, volume) and weekly summaries to that computer. If you turn on suggestions when linking, it also sends your routine names and exercises, the next 4 weeks of planned workouts and the exercise list, so JARVIS can suggest a routine or a schedule change; nothing changes until you approve it in the app. It never sends bodyweight, measurements, notes, photos or food. Scanning the JARVIS QR code uses the camera only while the scanner is open.
- **Purchases (optional).** In the Google Play version you can buy cosmetic items such as the Monarch Pass, or subscribe to the Monarch Club (renews monthly until you cancel in Google Play). Google Play handles the payment; the app never sees your card details. The app stores the product ID, order ID and purchase token on your device so it can unlock what you bought, and asks Google Play for your existing purchases when it starts or when you tap Restore purchases. Google's privacy policy covers the payment.
- **Update check.** When you tap "Check for updates", the app reads the public release list on GitHub. GitHub sees a normal web request.

The app never sends data in the background on its own, never sells data, and never shares it with advertisers.

## Permissions

- **Camera / photos:** only when you take or pick a progress photo.
- **Notifications and exact alarms:** rest-timer alerts and the reminders you turn on.
- **Install packages:** only to install an update you chose to download.
- **Biometrics:** only if you turn on the app lock.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Data → Erase all data deletes everything on the device, including photos and stored keys. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
